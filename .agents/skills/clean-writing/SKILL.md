# Clean Writing & Human Prose Standard

This skill governs all written prose, educational explanations, reports, documentation, and user-facing copy. Its purpose is to eliminate machine-like writing habits and produce clear, authoritative, human-grade text.

---

## 1. Strict Negative Constraints (Zero Tolerance)

### A. The Em Dash Ban
- **Zero em dashes (`—`) and en dashes (`–`)**: Never use an em dash or en dash anywhere in the text.
- **No hyphen substitutes**: Do not use `--` or ` - ` as a dash.
- **How to replace them**:
  - **Break into two sentences**: If a thought needs separation or emphasis, use a period.
  - **Use standard punctuation**: Use commas, parentheses, colons, or semicolons where syntactically appropriate.
  - **Use natural conjunctions**: Connect thoughts with `and`, `but`, `so`, `because`, or `although`.

```text
❌ WRONG:
The equation—derived from Newton's second law—shows that force increases with acceleration.

✅ CORRECT:
The equation is derived from Newton's second law. It shows that force increases with acceleration.
OR:
Derived from Newton's second law, the equation shows that force increases with acceleration.
```

### B. Banned AI Clichés & Buzzwords
Never use any of the following words or phrases:
- *delve into* / *delving*
- *testament to*
- *tapestry* / *rich tapestry*
- *beacon*
- *game-changer* / *revolutionize*
- *pivotal* / *paramount* / *crucial*
- *in conclusion* / *to summarize* / *in summary*
- *furthermore* / *moreover* (use "also", "in addition", or simply start the next fact)
- *it is worth noting* / *it is important to remember* / *note that* (when used as filler)
- *navigate* / *navigating the landscape*
- *harness* / *harnessing the power*
- *foster* / *fostering*
- *realm* / *sphere*
- *ever-evolving* / *fast-paced*
- *plethora* / *myriad*
- *cornerstone* / *keystone*
- *vibrant* / *bustling*
- *seamless* / *seamlessly*
- *embark* / *elevate* / *multifaceted*

### C. Banned Syntactic Formulas
- **The "Not only X, but also Y" / "It's not just X, it's Y" crutch**: State what something is directly without comparing it to what it isn't.
- **Setup-colon-payoff abuse**: Avoid ending every introductory clause with a colon followed by a bulleted payoff.
- **Forced triads**: Avoid forcing descriptions into artificial groups of three adjectives or nouns (e.g. "robust, scalable, and resilient").
- **Throat-clearing padding**: Eliminate opening phrases like *"In order to understand this concept, one must first look at..."* or *"When considering the subject of..."*. Jump straight to the subject.

## 2. Positive Style & Rhythm Guidelines

### A. Dynamic Sentence Length Variation
Avoid uniform sentence lengths. Mix punchy 3-word statements with longer, flowing explanations.

```text
Example Rhythm:
Work is done when a force moves an object. This is fundamental. If the object remains stationary, the displacement is zero, and no work occurs regardless of the applied effort.
```

### B. Active Voice & Concrete Subjects
- Place the actor and the action upfront.
- Avoid unnecessary passive constructions.

```text
❌ PASSIVE & WORDY:
A decision was made by the commission that the candidate should be disqualified.

✅ ACTIVE & DIRECT:
The commission disqualified the candidate.
```

### C. Saxon Simplicity over Latinate Puffery
Choose the simpler, more direct verb or noun:
- `use` instead of `utilize`
- `help` instead of `facilitate`
- `start` instead of `commence`
- `fix` instead of `rectify`
- `show` instead of `demonstrate` / `exemplify`
- `about` instead of `pertaining to`

## 3. Educational CBT Standards (UTME / WAEC / NECO)

When generating or reviewing explanations for examination questions:

### A. Key Point
- State the core governing theorem, rule, formula, or syllabus definition in one clear sentence.
- Include the exact formula or standard name if applicable.

### B. Body (Step-by-Step Proof)
- Walk through the problem algebraically or conceptually using numbered steps (`Step 1`, `Step 2`).
- Show the numbers and intermediate arithmetic so a candidate can follow the exact path to the solution.
- Keep the tone pedagogical, encouraging, and precise.

### C. Distractor Trap
- Explicitly diagnose why wrong options are tempting.
- Name the common misunderstanding (e.g. inverted fraction, forgotten negative sign, confusing simple plurality with absolute majority).
- Never use dismissive phrases like *"Option B is obviously false"*. Explain the cognitive error.

---

## 4. Verification Checklist

Before finalizing any text, verify:
1. Did I include any em dashes (`—`) or en dashes (`–`)? $\to$ **Zero allowed.**
2. Are there any banned AI filler words (e.g. *delve*, *testament*, *tapestry*, *furthermore*)? $\to$ **Remove them.**
3. Did I use active voice with varied sentence pacing? $\to$ **Yes.**
4. Is the opening sentence direct and free of throat-clearing? $\to$ **Yes.**
