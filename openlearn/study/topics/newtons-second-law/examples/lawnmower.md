# Example: Lawn mower (Newton’s Second Law)

**Goal:** Apply $a = F_{\text{net}}/m$ in one direction.

## Problem
A 24 kg lawn mower is pushed so that the net external force along the ground is 51 N in the forward direction. Friction is already accounted for in that net value. What is the acceleration?

## Steps
1. Identify knowns: $F_{\text{net}} = 51\,\text{N}$ (forward), $m = 24\,\text{kg}$.
2. Use $a = \frac{F_{\text{net}}}{m}$.
3. Compute: $a = \frac{51}{24} = 2.125\,\text{m/s}^2$ (forward).

## Answer
About $2.13\,\text{m/s}^2$ in the direction of the net external force.
