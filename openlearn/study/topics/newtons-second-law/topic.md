# Topic: Newton’s Second Law of Motion (Concept of a System)

**Learner outcome:** After this topic, you should be able to explain what Newton’s second law states, identify the system and net external force, and use $a = F_{\text{net}}/m$ (or $F_{\text{net}} = ma$) in simple one-direction calculations.
