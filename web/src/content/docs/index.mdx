## What this is

Every UTME candidate writes **Use of English plus three other subjects**, in two hours.
This API serves the past questions behind that, along with the syllabus they are drawn
from.

Two things make it different from a pile of PDFs:

- **Questions are keyed to subtopics.** A topic like *Equilibrium of forces* is too coarse
  to act on — is it the principle of moments you keep missing? Subtopics are small
  enough that "you are weak at *Change of Bases*" is a two-minute job, not a forty-question
  paper.
- **Explanations name the trap.** Every explanation carries the distractor that catches
  people. "You chose C because you confused the *declared* dividend with the *paid* one" is
  worth more than the correct answer alone.

## The shape of it

| | |
|---|---|
| **Catalog** | exams, subjects, syllabi — free, and **no key needed** |
| **Questions** | paged, metered, key required |
| **Exams** | JAMB, WAEC and NECO, each with its own subject names |

Subject names follow the exam that publishes them. JAMB writes **Use of English** and
**Art**; WAEC writes **English Language** and **Visual Arts**. They are different subjects,
not aliases of one — see [Syllabus and coverage](/docs/guides/syllabus/).
